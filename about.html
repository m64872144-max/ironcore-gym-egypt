Our Mission & Training Methodology

IronCore Egypt was established to deliver world-standard strength and athletic training in Egypt. We focus on natural bodybuilding, functional movement patterns, and scientific nutrition.

Core Training Focus:

  • Compound Strength: Squats, deadlifts, and bench presses.
  • Fat Loss & Cardio: Metabolic circuits and HIIT conditioning.
  • Mobility: Dynamic warmups, core stability, and posture correction.

Starting Your Routine in 3 Steps:

  1. Book your intake session and complete your InBody body composition scan.
  2. Receive your split program from your designated floor coach.
  3. Track your weights and progressive overload weekly.
